HIPPO generated a total of 4147 candidate structures, of which 630 (15.19%) were retained after applying identifiability and significance criteria. The distribution of fixed parameters shifted rightward by 1.60 on average (Cohen's d = 1.056) when comparing the retained subset to the full population, indicating a tendency towards simpler specifications. In terms of free parameters, the average complexity decreased from 7.201 to 5.603 (Δ = 1.598). Retention peaked among structures with approximately 10 fixed parameters (retention ≈ 80.0%), supporting the interpretation that over-parameterized variants were penalized by the diagnostics. The maximum admissible free-parameter count among retained models was Np = 9, yielding a complexity threshold of Np-2 = 7; the retained set contained 497 models below this threshold.
